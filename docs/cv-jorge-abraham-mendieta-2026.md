## Research

### Signal Propagation Properties in the *Drosophila melanogaster* Connectome

Preprint, not peer reviewed. Author and corresponding author. Analysis of the complete FlyWire v783 connectome with **138,639 neurons** and **15,091,983 synapses**, using SparseLTC and related parameter-free neural models with Maslov–Sneppen and community-preserving null models.
